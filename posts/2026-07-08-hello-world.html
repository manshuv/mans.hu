Hello, world

A note on the first post, and how this place is built.

By Manshu Verma · July 8, 2026

This is the first post on mans.hu — a place for notes, thoughts, and things worth writing down. Each post here is its own self-contained HTML page, sharing a common stylesheet for typography and layout.

Posts are free to add their own styling beyond the shared defaults when a topic calls for something more custom. There's no build step, no framework, and no database — just plain files, committed to git and deployed on push.
